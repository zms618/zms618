## Selected Research

### TG-WSPIS — ICME 2026

**Multi-Granular Text-Guided Weakly Supervised Pathology Image Segmentation**  
First author · IEEE International Conference on Multimedia & Expo (ICME), 2026

A text-guided weakly supervised pathology segmentation framework that uses multi-granular captions, soft image-text alignment, and collaborative learning to obtain fine-grained segmentation evidence without pixel-level supervision.

[Paper](https://zms618.github.io/papers/tg-wspis-icme-2026.pdf) · [Repository](https://github.com/zms618/TG-WSPIS)
